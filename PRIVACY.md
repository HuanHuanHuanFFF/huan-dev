# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Huan Dev is an instructions-only plugin. It does not operate an independent server, require an account, or independently collect, transmit, retain, or sell personal data.

When a user asks ChatGPT or Codex to apply a Huan Dev skill, the host product and user-authorized tools may access repositories, files, or services selected by the user. That access is controlled by the host product, the connected tool, and the user's approvals; their privacy terms apply. Huan Dev instructs the agent to preserve scope and authority, avoid unnecessary sensitive data, and disclose material evidence gaps.

Privacy questions may be submitted through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HuanHuanHuanFFF/huan-dev/issues).
